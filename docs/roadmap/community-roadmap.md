# Community Roadmap

High-level areas and ideas open for community contribution and discussion.

No implementation status here. For current progress and active tasks, see the internal project tracking.

---

## Post-launch: Investment Portfolio

A dedicated module for tracking financial investments:

- Basic portfolio view and tracking
- Passive income: dividends, interest, reinvested returns
- Asset revaluation logic
- Integration with Net Worth

---

## Post-launch: Advanced Asset Engine

Richer financial modeling for real assets:

- **Real estate**: mortgage engine (fixed and variable rate), property value evolution, rental income linked to assets
- **Financial assets**: advanced valuation logic, performance tracking

---

## Post-launch: Financial Simulator

"What-if" scenario engine for major financial decisions:

- Impact of buying a car, a property, or making a large investment
- Temporal projection (2–10 year horizon)
- Integration with Net Worth and Budget for realistic modeling

---

## Post-launch: Financial Goals Module

Personal financial objective tracking:

- Goal types: FIRE (financial independence), emergency fund, debt elimination, saving for a specific purchase
- Guidance system: what KPIs to prioritize, what levers to pull
- Coach integration: connect goals to phase scoring and recommendations

---

## Post-launch: Intelligent Assistants (AI / UX)

- Conversational assistant for quick data entry (speak or type four key values → movement logged)
- OCR support: photo of a bank statement → automatic movement parsing
- Annual close flow
- PWA support

---

## Open Ideas

Larger directions not yet scoped:

- Broader currency and regional inflation coverage
- Multi-device sync and offline-first experience
- Integrations with external financial data sources (open banking, broker APIs)
