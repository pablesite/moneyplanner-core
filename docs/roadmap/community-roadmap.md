# Community Roadmap

High-level areas and ideas open for community contribution and discussion.

No implementation status here. For current progress and active tasks, see the internal project tracking.

---

## Core Modules

Areas where the product is expected to grow over time:

- **Net Worth** — richer asset/liability tracking, historical evolution, entry UX improvements
- **Budget & Monthly Close** — tighter integration between daily movements, budget lines, and monthly results
- **Accounting & Daily Movements** — quick-entry UX, assisted input, importers from external tools
- **Financial Guide** — phase 5 scoring (financial independence), personal financial goals
- **Investment Portfolio** — basic portfolio view and tracking
- **Financial Simulator** — "what if" scenarios for major financial decisions
- **Family & Ownership** — ownership transfers, multi-member workflows

---

## Open Ideas

Larger directions not yet scoped:

- Conversational or assistant-style entry for daily movements
- Annual close flow
- PWA support
- Broader currency and regional inflation coverage
